## Known open comparisons

* Comparison uses exact normalized names (case, accents, and punctuation normalized), not fuzzy guesses: 17 of 78 prototype products matched one menu row, 61 did not, and none had an ambiguous exact match. Source rows without a confirmed match remain in the private reconciliation for human mapping.
* Of 115 menu rows, 51 availability values parse as unavailable, none parse as available, and 64 are blank or unrecognized. Among the 17 exact product matches, 5 availability values are recognized and all 5 say unavailable, which differs from the prototype. These values remain unapproved candidates.
* Of the 17 exact product matches, all have a candidate menu price; 6 differ from the prototype price. Eleven source category labels differ from their prototype category labels. These are discrepancy counts only; no source-derived price or commercial row is copied into this public repository.
* The menu workbook contains three duplicate normalized-name groups and no duplicate SKUs. The stock sheet contains 107 rows; 17 SKU-and-name matches were found for the exact menu matches, with no SKU/name conflicts. Ninety stock rows remain unmapped to a prototype product. Do not infer recipe control or sale price from those stock quantities.
* The menu workbook has fields for menu price, menu IVA, availability, images, weighable/unit, menu placement, IEPS, CFDI codes, and combo status. It has no fields defining modifier requirements/defaults/cardinality/price effects or recipe composition. Its image field is blank on all product rows.
* The current prototype contains 9 categories, 78 products, and 13 modifier groups. These counts describe only the prototype and must not be treated as the validated menu size.
* Money is represented as integer MXN centavos, as explicitly requested for this catalog. The seed's values still have prototype provenance and are not validated business prices; the EVL-111 IVA decision does not validate their amounts.
* Required-group defaults are explicit in the JSON because the current POS initializes a required group to its first option. That is a preservation of prototype behavior, not confirmation that the business wants that default.
* The seed lists certain products as unavailable. Those flags are preserved as prototype values pending source reconciliation.
