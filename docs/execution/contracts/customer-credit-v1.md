# EVL-135 implementation boundary

The human approved customer credit before pilot with owner-approved limits. Preserve anonymous sales and do not move the Linear milestone or write Linear. This worktree starts from reviewed integration287017e; stack codex/customers/evl-135 on codex/customers/credit-base. It includes the manual inventory ledger, split, refund and access slices, but not durable operational sync.

Implement minimal customer profiles with required display name and optional phone. Avoid unnecessary address, identification, staff credentials and birthday data. Maintain a documented manual-retention boundary; archiving profiles preserves linked accounting history. Do not promise a legal retention period or silently erase owed balances.

Keep debt/credit separate from prepaid credit balances. The default credit limit is zero and only the owner may grant or change a customer-specific limit, with a required reason and immutable actor/time event. An owner-approved numeric amount is an operator action, not a hardcoded business policy. Managers may perform permitted operational ledger actions; cashiers must not grant credit or adjust balances. Use the current canonical access matrix and add narrowly named actions/tests where this family needs an explicit permission.

Every charge, debt repayment, prepaid deposit/application, correction and reversal is an append-only centavo event with customer ID, stable command ID, original actor/time/reason and required sale/payment linkage where applicable. A command retry compares full business content. Changed content conflicts; source revisions and safe integer totals are checked before writes. Corrections append inverse entries linked to originals rather than deleting or changing history. Never refund change or count a payment as both a deposit and sale receipt.

Expose account statements, debt and available prepaid separately. Credit charges cannot exceed the owner-approved limit; debt repayments cannot over-repay unless an explicit separate prepaid deposit is chosen. Prepaid application cannot exceed available prepaid or the sale's remaining amount. Full/partial application must be part of an atomic checkout family; until EVL-126 provides that transaction, keep the checkout integration gate explicit rather than reporting a completed durable credit sale. Do not introduce disconnected fake settlement buttons or claim remote sync success.

As with inventory, save-before-success, stable retry identity, current role/actor checks at confirmation, fail-closed corrupt storage, preservation of newer unrelated saved POS data, Spanish operator errors and accessible controls are required. Add domain and actual UI tests against these behaviors. Commit bounded checkpoints and document what is implemented versus pending126 server/local atomicity. Root reviews with fixes before publication/integration; drafts only and main unchanged.
