# EVL-185 checkpoint: paid orders remain in kitchen

This bounded runtime checkpoint separates the pending-account list from the kitchen queue in the current local POS prototype. A successful checkout removes the account from `open` while preserving its kitchen ticket and immutable item text (including modifier display and notes). The Comanda reads and advances `kitchenTickets` independently; each action re-reads and validates the persisted ticket before writing, so a stale card cannot restore a cancellation or erase a newly added ticket. Delivery and authorized cancellation remove a ticket from the active queue. Payment retries are guarded by the in-flight checkout state, the stable folio, an existing completed-sale check, and a source snapshot captured when checkout opens and compared against both live and persisted account state before the sale is committed.

Cancellation through the POS checks the actor's current local role when the dialog opens and again when it is confirmed. A required reason, actor, timestamp, and cancellation audit entry are stored with the ticket and cancellation sale record. This is a prototype UI guard; it is not trusted server-side authorization.

Verification covers synthetic paid orders in queued, preparing, ready, and delivered states, including POS and kitchen reloads, one successful sale for a double-clicked payment action, and domain tests for lifecycle transitions and cancellation audit metadata.

EVL-185 remains incomplete. The app still uses browser local storage, so this does not establish durable or multi-device storage, trusted authorization, production payment settlement, conflict handling across devices, or cashier/hardware behavior. Full order/payment contract integration, persistence, backend authorization, and operational acceptance remain gated by EVL-113/116/118/126 and related payment and kitchen issues.
