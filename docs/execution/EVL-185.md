# EVL-185 checkpoint: paid orders remain in kitchen

This bounded runtime checkpoint separates the pending-account list from the kitchen queue in the current local POS prototype. A successful checkout removes the account from `open` while preserving its kitchen ticket and immutable item text (including modifier display and notes). The Comanda reads and advances `kitchenTickets` independently; delivery and authorized cancellation remove a ticket from the active queue. Payment retries are guarded by the in-flight checkout state, the stable folio, an existing completed-sale check, and a comparison against the source open-account snapshot before the sale is committed.

Cancellation through the POS checks the actor's current local role when the dialog opens and again when it is confirmed. A required reason, actor, timestamp, and cancellation audit entry are stored with the ticket and cancellation sale record. This is a prototype UI guard; it is not trusted server-side authorization.

Verification covers synthetic paid orders in queued, preparing, ready, and delivered states, including POS and kitchen reloads, one successful sale for a double-clicked payment action, and domain tests for lifecycle transitions and cancellation audit metadata.

EVL-185 remains incomplete. The app still uses browser local storage, so this does not establish durable or multi-device storage, trusted authorization, production payment settlement, conflict handling across devices, or cashier/hardware behavior. Full order/payment contract integration, persistence, backend authorization, and operational acceptance remain gated by EVL-113/116/118/126 and related payment and kitchen issues. Demo seed values and source-derived commercial values are not approved business data.
