## Real-period UI and export checkpoint

`report-period.ts` resolves explicit local branch times to UTC, uses real branch-calendar boundaries for today/seven/thirty days, and refuses impossible or repeated clock times unless the caller chooses their occurrence. Custom end times are exclusive. Legacy display labels and impossible normalized dates remain unknown. Financial totals use each sale/return's own event time: a refund today for an older sale can produce negative net receipts today. Tips remain separate from gross receipts, cancellation captures do not count as receipts, and unknown dates/methods remain visible as unresolved evidence rather than guessed values.

The Reports screen now offers Hoy, Últimos 7 días, Últimos 30 días, and a custom local date/time interval, with an explicit IANA branch time zone. A repeated custom wall-clock hour requires the operator to choose the earlier or later occurrence; nonexistent and rejected ambiguous times disable totals and export with a Spanish explanation. The selected period and UTC boundaries are shown before download, and the export uses the same resolved interval as the dashboard.

Receipts and tips are counted by captured sale/payment time; refund and void movements are counted by their own captured event time. Thus a refund today for an older sale appears in today's returns/net totals and export without bringing the old receipt into today's period. Unknown-date legacy sales and compensations remain visible in a separate history table/export section; the app does not infer an instant from a display date, day label, or normalized timestamp. Invalid records fail the period projection closed, and the current `viewReports` permission is checked again at export.
