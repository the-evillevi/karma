# EVL-186 checkpoint: cash received, change, and net payments

The checkout calculates MXN money as integer centavos. Contract-shaped payment records store `netAmountCents` and `tipCents`; the compatibility `amount`/`amountCents` fields also hold the net applied to the sale. A separate tender ledger retains every positive received amount, including a cash row returned in full with zero net, together with its change and net allocation. Returned change is deducted from cash only, net records are non-negative and sum to the amount due, and per-payment tip centavos sum to the separately recorded sale tip. Method reports aggregate payment net amounts, and the sale detail exposes each tender and returned change.

Legacy sales have only amount-valued payment rows and no reliable tender/change provenance. Their recorded amount remains the report fallback; no historical change is inferred or silently subtracted. New sale payment amounts are derived from the centavo net amount for compatibility with existing readers.

Synthetic tests cover $50 cash against $100 tender, exact cash, cash/card split with change returned only from cash, tips, non-cash overpayment, insufficient cash to fund mixed-method change, negative/fractional/unsafe entries, 29-cent precision, and conservative legacy reading. Component coverage drives a $50 checkout with $100 cash through the real POS and confirms the persisted net and reported method total are $50.

This does not add a real spreadsheet exporter: the current Exportar a Excel control remains a simulation. New `payments.amount` and `amountCents` values are net for downstream consumers. Production persistence and authorization remain gated by EVL-116/118/126, and terminal settlement remains outside this prototype change.
