# EVL-123 bounded checkout slice

## Checkpoint

This change extends the reviewed EVL-186 centavo tender implementation in the prototype checkout. It adds the 20% tip suggestion, accepts an arbitrary MXN tip amount to centavos, shows the amount's equivalent percentage of the discounted subtotal, and permits manually captured cash, card, and transfer rows while offline. Card and transfer rows persist `recordMode: "manual"` and `verificationStatus: "manual_unverified"`; cash is marked `not_applicable`. Sales also record the overall external-payment verification state. Checkout/result copy and the reopened Reports sale detail show that external authorization was not verified. Older card/transfer records without verification metadata are labeled as unknown, never as approved.

No terminal request or bank response is simulated. Card and transfer records are manual operator records only, whether connected or offline. EVL-133 owns automatic Mercado Pago integration. Tip and tender totals continue to use integer MXN centavos; split tender records reconcile to the order total. Completing payment leaves an unpaid-for-preparation kitchen ticket in its existing state.

## Verification

- Component regression: custom MXN 7.25 tip on a 60.00 base displays 12.08%, closes as 6,725 cents, and saves 725 tip cents exactly.
- Component regression: 20% tip on a 60.00 base closes a 72.00 mixed cash/card/transfer order while offline, writes one completed sale despite a double click, marks card/transfer manual and unverified, and preserves its queued kitchen ticket and notes.
- Component regression: after remounting POS and reopening Reports, the saved manual/unverified notice remains visible; a legacy card sale without status metadata is reported as unknown.
- The previously reviewed EVL-186 checks cover exact cash tender/change, zero-net returned cash tender history, report totals, and malformed-input recovery.

## Acceptance status and open gates

The bounded prototype behavior above is implemented. It does not complete EVL-123's trusted close, durable persistence, or authorized correction requirements. Browser local storage remains prototype persistence; EVL-126 durable sync and EVL-118 trusted authorization remain open. Automatic terminal authorization/settlement remains EVL-133; post-close correction/refund/void flows remain EVL-125. Report export remains EVL-130. No real bank authorization, settlement, hardware behavior, or production durability is claimed.
