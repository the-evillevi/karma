# EVL-128 · Typed manual inventory ledger

## Implemented in this slice

Inventory balances now come from a typed local ledger using integer base units: grams (`g`), millilitres (`ml`), and pieces (`pz`). Kilograms and litres convert exactly to their base units, quantities are limited to 32 characters and three decimal places, and fractional pieces or unsafe totals are rejected.

The POS can record positive stock entries, positive waste quantities that reduce stock, signed manual adjustments, and audited low-stock threshold changes. Each event stores a stable command ID, actor, timestamp, reason, item revision, unit and quantity snapshots, and an explicit local synchronization state. The current role and latest stored item revision are checked at confirmation. Storage failure leaves the form and its command identity available for retry; a successful retry produces one event.

The seed catalog’s current balances are written once as explicit synthetic opening entries. They are marked unverified and require a physical count. The old text-only demonstration movement history remains visible as clearly labeled fixture data and does not affect ledger balances. The existing seeded article list remains the catalog source for this slice.

## Verification

- `src/inventory/inventory-ledger.test.mjs` checks exact conversions, integer base-unit math, opening balances, movement signs, threshold changes and retry identity, revision conflicts, unsafe values, audit-text integrity, and rejection of unknown stored fields.
- `src/inventory/inventory-ui.test.jsx` exercises opening-ledger initialization and reload, stock-low thresholds, typed entries, actor/time/reason capture, fractional piece rejection, reason bounds, role changes, stale item revisions, failed-write retry, manager authorization, cashier read-only access, and preservation of newer POS records.
- `pnpm test:domain`, `pnpm test:components`, `pnpm build`, and `pnpm build:pwa` are the scoped project checks for this change.

## Remaining acceptance gates

- Item creation and editing are not included. The operator UI still uses the existing seeded item catalog and does not yet let an operator add, rename, reclassify, or archive inventory items.
- These records are local-only. Cross-device synchronization, server authority, durable conflict resolution, and retry acknowledgement remain dependent on EVL-126 and EVL-129. `pending` means locally recorded and awaiting synchronization; it does not mean a remote write succeeded.
- Recipe consumption and automatic inventory deductions are not implemented. The recipe screen remains informational and never changes stock.
- The synthetic opening balances must be replaced or confirmed by a physical stock count before the ledger is treated as verified business history.
