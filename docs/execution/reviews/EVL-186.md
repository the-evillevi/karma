# EVL-186 root review and fixes

Reviewed 74a8cd3 against the actual issue. A 50 MXN sale with 100 MXN received now records 50 MXN change and 50 MXN net cash. Checkout calculations, new payment rows and method totals use integer centavos. Mixed payments deduct change only from cash, never from card or transfer; tip attribution remains separate and net payments sum to the amount due.

## Findings fixed

- Critical: filtering zero-net cash rows discarded the entire tender/change history when cash was fully returned. A separate tender ledger now retains every positive tender, including net zero; net payment records and reports exclude returned money. The component regression uses 50 MXN card plus 20 MXN cash returned in full and verifies the saved tender ledger.
- Warning: parsing a blank or invalid amount in the remaining-payment action could throw into the app diagnostic boundary. Completing the remainder now safely fills the selected row, while invalid other rows continue to block confirmation. Malformed saved prices/quantities refuse checkout with a repair message instead of an exception.

## Independent evidence

Root reran all 20 component tests, 60 unit tests without skips and the four-entry build in the payments worktree. The actual POS/report/reload test covers the original 50/100/50 reproduction. Focused unit cases cover exact cash, split tender, multiple cash rows, tip conservation, non-cash overpayment, malformed and unsafe centavos, and old amount-only records.

## Acceptance limits

No blocking findings remain in this bounded app fix. Excel export remains simulated and is not accepted; EVL-130 must use the net-centavo contract. Old history has no reliable tender/change evidence, so its recorded amount is preserved without invented historical change. Trusted authorization and durable production storage remain EVL-118/126 gates. No real terminal operation or settlement was verified. EVL-186 stays open pending those requirements.
