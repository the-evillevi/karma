# EVL-136 birthday profile and eligibility preview

## Implemented in this candidate

- Customer birth date is optional and is recorded with the separately versioned `profile.birthday.set.v1` event. Existing `profile.create` and `profile.update` payloads remain `{ name, phone }`; replay does not infer dates for historical profiles.
- The append-only birthday policy ledger starts disabled. Only a currently authorized owner can save a policy event, with a stable command ID, actor, time, reason, and expected revision. The owner must explicitly choose a branch IANA time zone, exact-day/window rule, February 29 rule, and a product, category, or integer-centavo maximum benefit before enabling it.
- Eligibility is an explanation-only preview evaluated against a strict branch-local calendar date. It rejects future birth dates and invalid timestamps, explains disabled/missing/out-of-window cases, and exposes no reservation or redemption action.
- Owner/manager profile permission controls birthday visibility and editing. Owner permission is checked again when saving policy; identity/role loss closes the capture. The customer and policy records fail closed on malformed local history, and write failures leave their forms open for retry.

## Remaining acceptance gates

This is a local profile/policy and preview slice. It does not redeem or reserve benefits, enforce one redemption per birthday cycle, apply a product/discount, create a free sale line, or claim server acknowledgement. EVL-126 must provide the atomic sale/customer transaction, current server authorization, uniqueness constraint, idempotent retries, cross-device reconciliation, authorized exception history, and separate reporting before any birthday benefit can be consumed. Local `local_pending` policy events are not remote proof.

No real benefit amount, product, category, window, timezone, or leap-day behavior is supplied by the code; the owner configures those values. A maximum covered amount is stored as integer centavos. Product/category records capture operator-selected IDs and display-name snapshots, but this preview does not claim that a live catalog item exists or is available.

## Focused verification

- Domain tests cover strict birth dates and UTC instants, disabled/incomplete policy validation, stable policy retries and changed-content conflicts, stale revision/current-owner checks, Mexico City UTC boundaries, year-crossing windows, explicit February 29 rules, and future birth dates.
- Mounted customer tests cover legacy profile event compatibility, birthday capture and local history, owner policy configuration/preview, failed-write retry, and policy dialog closure when owner authority is lost.
