# EVL-122 — Open accounts, order details, and table settings

Open accounts now save captured line prices, type, reference, creator/responsible person, creation time, total in centavos, and any delivery or pickup details. The station restores those values after reload and resume. Changing the order type leaves the captured lines and prices in place. Account totals are still derived from captured line prices and discounts; the saved centavo total is a snapshot for the unpaid account.

Delivery orders require a recipient name, phone, and address before saving, sending the comanda, or checkout. Pickup orders (Para llevar or Recoger) require a name and phone. A quick local cash sale has no customer requirement.

Dueña and Encargado can set 1–50 available tables, with a default of 12. Mesa selection is optional. Lowering the count leaves existing account references intact and offers an out-of-range saved table as a historical option. The table-count mutation calls `requireAction('configureTables')` when the foundation runtime supplies it; the seeded demo retains its role-based fallback.

The current app storage remains browser-local. This slice adds no cross-device versioning or conflict resolution. Accounts marked with a sync conflict remain pending and their actions are disabled until EVL-126 provides a storage and reconciliation contract. Secure role enforcement and durable recovery remain open until EVL-118/126 are integrated and independently accepted. Move/merge remain deferred, and product-level split selection remains with EVL-155.
